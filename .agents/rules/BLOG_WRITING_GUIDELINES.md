# Blog Guidelines & Writing Standards for Ramam Textiles

This document specifies the mandatory requirements for all blog posts, articles, and journal drafts for Ramam Textiles.

## 1. Paragraph Length
- Every paragraph MUST be under 3 lines (typically 1 to 2 sentences, 25 to 45 words max).
- Break long thoughts into bite-sized units.
- Never write dense walls of text.

## 2. Heading Structure
- **H1 (Article Title)**: One H1 per article. Must incorporate the primary target keyword naturally and spark strong search interest.
- **H2 (Main Sections)**: Top-level subheadings under H1. Formats major themes or chronological phases.
- **H3 (Sub-sections)**: Detailed subheadings nested under H2. Break down specific nuances, step numbers, or sub-topics.

## 3. Bullet Points
- Use bullet points wherever appropriate.
- Bullet points should be concise, skimmable, and high-impact.
- Use them for lists of materials, pros/cons, care instructions, technical specs, and key takeaways.

## 4. Add a Relevant Table
- Every blog post MUST include a structured data table.
- Table types:
  - Technical fabric specifications (GSM, yarn count, shrinkage, opacity).
  - Comparison matrices (Technique A vs Technique B).
  - Wash & care temperature/detergent guides.
  - Sourcing MOQ, pricing brackets, and lead times.
- Tables must have clean header cells and clear, readable rows.

## 5. Images or Infographics
- Every blog post MUST include relevant visuals or an infographic process flow.
- Formats:
  - High-resolution workshop and artisan craft photos.
  - Step-by-step visual workflow / process stage cards.
  - Informative captions describing the technique or product.

## 6. Author Details
- Prominently include author information:
  - Author Name (e.g. Master Craftsman Guild, Ramam Textiles Technical Desk).
  - Professional Role / Title.
  - Verified Artisan Hallmark / Credentials.
  - Brief author bio describing deep craft experience.

## 7. FAQ Section
- Every blog post MUST include a dedicated Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) section.
- Minimum 3 to 5 questions based on actual Google "People Also Ask" queries.
- Answers must be direct, authoritative, and concise (< 3 lines per paragraph).
- JSON-LD FAQPage schema markup must be generated for rich search results.

## 8. Top 5 Competitor Blog Analysis
- Before finalizing any blog, search the primary keyword on Google.
- Open and read the top 5 ranking articles on the topic.
- Extract their core headings, primary keywords, strengths, and weak spots.
- Deliver superior depth, practical artisan insights, and original data not found on generic affiliate blogs.

## 9. Lead Generation CTA (Redirect to Contact Page)
- Every blog post MUST feature a high-conversion lead generation CTA block.
- Primary Action: Link directly to the contact page (`/contact`) with clear label:
  - "Contact Atelier / Start Sourcing"
  - "Inquire About Custom Manufacturing"
  - "Get In Touch With Our Jaipur Workshop"
- Secondary Action: Quick Quote trigger / WhatsApp direct line.

---

## 4-Step Blog Writing Workflow
1. **Step 1: Search the primary keyword on Google.**
2. **Step 2: Open and read the top 5 ranking blogs.**
3. **Step 3: Finalise the blog outline and structure (H1, H2, H3, table, bullets, author, FAQs, CTA).**
4. **Step 4: Write the blog with Claude and humanise it.** Ensure an authentic, conversational yet authoritative artisan voice.
