Authentic Jaipur Hand Block Printing & Craftsmanship

Rooted in the historic textile settlements of Sanganer and Bagru in Rajasthan, Ramam Textiles preserves over four centuries of traditional Indian artisan printing. Master block carvers hand-chisel seasoned Teak and Sheesham wood blocks with intricate floral bootis, imperial Mughal jals, and geometric trellises. Utilizing natural botanical mordanting, harda tannin priming, and certified AZO-free pigments or natural fermented indigo vats, our artisans stamp each meter of combed cotton with rhythmic accuracy and sustainable care. Every fabric piece undergoes sun drying and pure river wash treatments to ensure long-lasting colorfastness and luxurious softness.
